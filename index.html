Edition VIII, Heel

Chapter XIV goes back to the first name, which chapter I covered in two lines. Kobus is a short form of Jacobus, which is Latin for the Hebrew Yaʿaqov, whose Biblical explanation is a heel and whose scholarly alternative is a protection. The chapter follows the chain, lists the other Kobuses that could be sourced, and says plainly how much could not be found: no first written Kobus, no earliest bearer at the Cape, no sourced figure for how common the name is. Read the chapter. The look moves to Delft blue and cream with a tile-border under the nav, and this edition's instrument lets you wear the name down, step by step, along two roads. The full log, and the open questions.
