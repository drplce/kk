Edition VII, Registration

Chapter XIII follows up a line from Rafe Lavine's own ten records: a 1968 album by a British trio he called unsung heroes with, he said, a cult following in South Africa. The trio and the record are real and well documented; the South African following, searched every way, is not. Read the chapter. The look shifts to a Hammond's brass and petrol-teal, and this edition's instrument is a working drawbar registration you can set and sound yourself. The full log, and the open questions.
