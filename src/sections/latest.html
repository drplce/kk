Edition X, Tiles

Chapter V said the name scores 26 in Scrabble and stopped. Chapter XVIII asks what that number assumes. The name needs two K tiles and a set holds one, so on a real board it is worth 21, and neither half of it is a word a dictionary accepts, though KOB, the antelope, is. A working board lets you place any word and score it by the premium squares, with blanks. The research was all search extracts, and the chapter lists what they could not say: why a K is worth five, what the Afrikaans edition’s tiles are, and what Collins thinks of any of it. The look moves to board green with a row of tiles under the nav. Play the name. The full log, and the open questions.
