Edition IX, Render

A family phrase with no known origin became Volume IV: Pig Man Fat, or PMF. The archive and the web were searched and found nothing, so this edition does something the rest of the site never does and makes it up. Chapter XV tells the legend of a thin pig, a wolf, a jackal and the last pat of fat in a drought, after Br’er Rabbit and the Cape’s Jackal and Wolf tales. Chapter XVI retells it twelve times and watches it grow. Chapter XVII lists what is real behind it. The fiction is labelled as fiction on every page it appears. The look moves to lard cream, cured red and rendered gold, with a drip border under the nav and headings set in a fat face. Read the legend. The full log, and the open questions.
