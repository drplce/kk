Pig Man Fat

PMF

First the true origin of the name, as a supplied essay tells it: a radio quiz producer surnamed Fat, nicknamed Pigman. Then an invented tall tale about a thin pig, a wolf, a jackal and the last pat of fat in a district that did not exist, told once in full and retold twelve times.

Chapters XV and XVI are invented. The rest of the site reports what could be checked, and so does chapter XVIII, the true origin of Pig Man Fat, which reports an essay supplied and confirmed by the owner of this site. Pig Man Fat was first searched for in the owner's project archive and on the open web, and nothing was found, so the legend in chapters XV and XVI was made up for this edition, in the manner of Br’er Rabbit and of the Jackal and Wolf tales of the Cape. It was made up before the true origin arrived, and nothing in it comes from the essay. Every teller, place, rhyme and number in the legend and the retelling is fiction. Chapter XVII holds the real facts about the traditions it borrows from, and what was checked and what was not.
