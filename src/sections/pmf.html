Pig Man Fat

PMF

A tall tale about a thin pig, a wolf, a jackal and the last pat of fat in a district that did not exist. It is told once in full, then retold twelve times to show what tellings do to a story.

Read this first

The legend in this volume is invented. The rest of the site reports what could be checked. This volume does not. Pig Man Fat was searched for in the owner's project archive and on the open web, and nothing was found, so the story below was made up for this edition, in the manner of Br’er Rabbit and of the Jackal and Wolf tales of the Cape. Every teller, place, rhyme and number in chapters XV and XVI is fiction. Chapter XVII is where the real facts live, with what was checked and what was not.
