How this site grows

Every edition keeps the bones and changes the body. Once a week, on its own, the site chooses one thing already on it that deserves a deeper look, sends out research, builds what comes back into a new or expanded section, changes its visual treatment, and adds one interactive element. The log below is the record.

Open questions

Threads a future edition could pull. Each one is real, and each one is unanswered on these pages today.

  • What was Rafe Lavine's afternoon programme on SAfm, and in which years did it run?
  • Was the mystery-song feature really called Beat the Beat, and did it have rules, prizes or a regular slot?
  • What date was the broadcast? Ed, Trevor Worley, Martin Lake, Charles Mitchell and Ben were the other callers that day.
  • The Average White Band recorded Pick Up the Pieces at Criteria in Miami with Arif Mardin. What happened in that room, and who played the sax solo?
  • Kobus Wiese, Kobus Moolman, and the other Kobuses: how far does the name travel, and where did it start?
  • The Ó Cinnéide of Munster: how did a nephew of Brian Boru's clan become a surname carried to the Cape?
  • Bodoni Moda is the face of these pages. What did Giambattista Bodoni's K actually look like, and how close is this one?
  • What did SAfm sound like in the 1990s, before the 2004 cull, and who else was on it?
  • The Peddlers' Three in a Cell got Lavine into jazz. Who were The Peddlers, and why did they have a cult following in South Africa?
  • Radio Today, 1485 AM, and The RockFest: what was community rock radio in Johannesburg in 2006?
